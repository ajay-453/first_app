The problem

Your weather app is guessing about a city. Not about you.

Forecasts are modeled for a region the size of an airport — not the trailhead, the campsite, or the balcony you're standing on. Microclimates can swing 8°C and flip from clear to storm within a mile. Stratos closes that gap.

Meet Stratos

Five sensors. One on-device brain.

🌡️

Real local readings

A medical-grade sensor array tracks temperature, humidity, barometric pressure, UV and particulate air quality, updated every 10 seconds.

🧠

AI that learns your spots

An edge model fuses your live readings with regional data to forecast the next 12 hours — and gets sharper at the places you visit most.

🔋

7-day battery

Low-power design runs a full week on a charge and tops up over USB-C in 40 minutes. No app-draining background nonsense.

📡

Works off-grid

Bluetooth to your phone in town; standalone e-ink readout on the trail. Your forecast doesn't need a signal.

🎒

Built to be carried

42 grams, IP67 water- and dust-proof, with a carabiner clip. Lives on your bag, not in a drawer.

🔔

Storm alerts

A pressure drop that signals incoming weather pings your phone before the clouds roll in.

How it works

From thin air to a forecast in three steps.

Sense

The sensor array samples the air around you continuously — the conditions where you actually are.

Predict

The on-device AI blends your readings with regional models to project the next 12 hours, locally.

Notify

See it on the e-ink display or your phone, and get a heads-up the moment conditions are about to turn.

Questions

Good to know

How accurate is the forecast?

Because Stratos measures conditions at your exact location, its 12-hour outlook is dramatically tighter than region-wide models — especially in mountains, near water, and in dense cities where microclimates dominate.

Does it need my phone?

No. The built-in e-ink display shows current conditions and the forecast standalone. Pair with the phone app for history, trends and storm alerts when you have a connection.

What's the battery life, really?

About 7 days of normal use per charge, and a full recharge over USB-C in roughly 40 minutes.

When will it ship?

Estimated February–March 2027 depending on tier. We'll post monthly production updates to all backers.
